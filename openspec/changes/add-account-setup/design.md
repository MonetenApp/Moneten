# Design

## Context

Issue #85 builds the FinTS path of the assistant on the connector of issue #13 part 1 ([add-fints-banking design](../add-fints-banking/design.md)). The services of extensions under "Other" come with E6 of the extension plan. Issue #86 added "Save PIN" to the login step. [ADR 0014](../../../docs/decisions/0014-fints-through-hbci4java.md) and [ADR 0009](../../../docs/decisions/0009-database-password-as-in-moneymoney.md) apply.

## Decisions

### The data of an access

`Storage` keeps each access in the table `access`: the source as JSON, the bank name, the login name, and the opaque `AccessState` of its connector. An account links to its access with `accessID` and to the account at the bank with `remoteID`, and keeps `refreshedAt` and `refreshError` for the status of the account-refresh spec. An access goes with its last account: `Store.deleteAccount(_:)` deletes its row and state, and the app forgets its PIN. Migration `v7` adds this and changes no existing row. [data-model.md](../../../docs/data-model.md#bank-accesses) lists the fields.

### Who owns what

- `Domain`: `BankEntry` reads an IBAN or a Bankleitzahl and runs the IBAN checks. `Ledger.setupMatch(for:listed:)` and `Ledger.accountsToAdd(_:listed:connecting:)` decide which listed accounts the setup adds.
- `FinTS`: `FinTSConnector.bankDirectory` reads the bank directory, and `listAccounts` logs in and lists the accounts. `BankLookup` keeps the bank of the entry on screen: a new entry drops the bank of the last one, and a lookup that ends after the entry changed is ignored, so a quick "Next" never logs in to the bank of an earlier entry. `LoginHint` holds what a bank calls its login name.
- `Sync`: `SetupSession` is the session of the login. It answers the PIN request with the PIN of the login step. A rejected PIN ends the login, so the message of the bank shows in the login step and the bank gets no second attempt. Every other request opens the dialogs of `SessionBroker`, one at a time with the dialogs of running refreshes.
- `Storage`: `Store.add(_:state:accounts:)` writes the access and its accounts in one transaction.
- `App`: `AccountSetup` holds the steps and their state, and `AccountSetupSheet` shows them. A TAN dialog opens as a sheet on top of the assistant.

### Seams for later work

- "Other" lists `SetupService.menu`: the services of the installed extensions by name, each with its own login step, and "Offline account / Cash" last. `AppModel` picks the connector of an access by its source.
- After the setup, `SessionBroker.remember(_:for:save:)` keeps the PIN in memory, so the first refresh does not ask again, and saves it in the database when the user turned on "Save PIN" in the login step. The bank accepted it at the login of the setup.

### The search for a bank

The helper sends the whole bank directory of hbci4java once per assistant, with the command `banks`: 4,063 Bankleitzahlen in 535 KB, in about 0.2 seconds. `BankDirectory` keeps it in memory, so no keystroke starts a helper. An entry that starts like an IBAN, or has 8 or more digits, goes to `BankEntry`. Any other entry is a search, and the digits of the start of a Bankleitzahl count as one word also when they are typed in groups, such as "760 30". A `failed` line of the helper shows its text.

A search compares each word of the entry with the words of the name and the place, in both spellings of umlauts ("muenchen" and "munchen" for "München") and without accents, and with the start of the Bankleitzahl. Every word must match. The order is:

1. Banks with a FinTS address before the others. The entry of a merged bank, such as "Sparkasse Bonn -alt-", comes after them too, because the bank may use another Bankleitzahl for online banking now.
2. A bank whose name starts with the entry, such as "ING-DiBa" for "ing" before a bank in Ingolstadt.
3. A bank where each word starts a word of the name or the place.
4. A bank where a word only appears inside a word, such as "sparkasse" in "Stadtsparkasse".
5. A Bankleitzahl with a login hint, so that "comdirect" offers the four Bankleitzahlen that comdirect names before the four others.
6. The name, then the place and the Bankleitzahl, so two banks of one name stand in the order of their places.

The directory names some banks differently from their customers, such as "Deutsche Kreditbank Berlin" for DKB, so the short name of each bank with a login hint is a search word too. A search takes 4 to 12 ms in a release build and runs off the main thread. The step shows the first 50 matches. Each row has the place and the Bankleitzahl, and the step says that the Bankleitzahl is digits 5 to 12 of the IBAN, because the login sends the Bankleitzahl that the user chooses, and Consorsbank, for example, has two.

### A Bankleitzahl of a merged bank

The bank directory of hbci4java has no field for a replaced Bankleitzahl. It marks the entry of a merged bank with "-alt-" at the end of the name, such as "Sparkasse Bonn -alt-": 279 of its 4,063 entries, and one more with "-alt" without the last hyphen. The mark does not say that the Bankleitzahl is outdated. During a merger the Bundesbank lets a bank keep two Bankleitzahlen in one place, and "-alt-" only tells them apart: 77 of the 280 are still active in the Bankleitzahlen file of the Bundesbank, and 48 of those have a FinTS address. So the app never refuses such an entry and never says that it is outdated. `BankLookup` accepts it like any bank with a FinTS address, and the first step shows `BankDirectory.Entry.mergerNote`: "This Bankleitzahl belongs to a bank that merged. If the login fails, ask your bank which Bankleitzahl it uses for online banking now." An entry of a merged bank without a FinTS address gets the message of any bank without one.

### Supported banks

"Supported Banks..." in the Help menu opens a window of its own. `SupportedBanks` holds the bank directory and the services of the extensions that may run, the same services that "Other" lists. A search uses the words of the first step: the banks in the order of `BankDirectory.search(_:)`, and a service when every word is in its name or in the name of its extension. The list shows only what the app reaches: a bank with a FinTS address, with "FinTS/HBCI with TAN method" and its note, which is the help of its login hint or the note of a merged bank, and each service with the name of its extension. When the bank directory does not load, the window says so above the services. It shows at most 500 banks, and says how many match when there are more, so that the window stays quick. The rows are a lazy stack in a scroll view, not a SwiftUI list: the table behind a list reported a reentrant operation of its delegate when a search changed a whole section and hundreds of rows. So the stack does what the table did for the keyboard and for VoiceOver. Down Arrow in the search field selects the first row, Tab leads from the search field into the rows, and Down Arrow, Up Arrow, Page Down, Page Up, Home, and End move the selection and scroll it into view. Up Arrow on the first row goes back to the search field. A key with Command, Option, Control, or Shift goes to the system, so Command-Down Arrow moves the cursor in the search field. `SupportedBanksKeys` decides what each key does. Each row is one element for VoiceOver: the name as its label, and its place and Bankleitzahl, its access method, and its whole note as its value. Each row has the same height, with lines that are cut at the end and the whole note in its tooltip. Without a search the banks come by name, then by place, whatever the order of the bank directory.

### Login hints

`LoginHint` is a table in `Sources/FinTS/LoginHints.swift`: a label for the login name field, one sentence of help, and the URL of the public page of the bank that confirms it. A hint covers the Bankleitzahlen that a page of the bank names as its own: 76030080 for Consorsbank, 12030000 for DKB, the four that comdirect names, and 50010517 for ING. The FinTS server is no rule: DAB BNP Paribas (70120400) uses the server of Consorsbank, and no page of the bank confirms that its login is the same, so it gets no hint. The same holds for the four other Bankleitzahlen of comdirect in the directory. The savings banks are too many for a list and have a host each, so a savings bank is a bank with "Sparkasse" in its name on the FinTS servers of the savings banks, whose host label before ".de" starts with "s-fints-pt-" or is "s-hbci". That rule leaves out a Bausparkasse and the Südtiroler Sparkasse, which uses the servers of the cooperative banks. It covers the entry of a merged savings bank, because the setup goes on with it. The link of the savings banks is the guide of Ostseesparkasse Rostock and says so, because no page of the savings banks as a group describes FinTS. A hint without a public page of the bank is left out.

The cooperative banks on the FinTS servers of Atruvia (`.atruvia.de` and `.bankenit.de`) are many too, so they get the hint "VR-NetKey or alias" by their host, with the page of the Volksbanken Raiffeisenbanken as the source. A Sparda-Bank on these servers calls its login name the Sparda-NetKey, and the Südtiroler Sparkasse is no cooperative bank, so both keep "User ID". These servers also take a VR-Kennung, "VRK" and digits, only in capitals: the login step sends and stores it in capitals and without spaces, and so does the connector for an access that stored it in lower case before. The helper takes the login name of the command over the user ID of the stored state, which hbci4java would otherwise send. apoBank does the same. `FinTSServer` in `Domain` holds this form, because `Access.isSameLogin` compares FinTS login names in it, so a second setup of the same VR-Kennung in another case joins the access that the app has. A login name of more than 30 characters, the length of the user ID in FinTS, fails on these servers before any connection, with the advice to change the alias in the online banking first. `Sources/FinTS/CooperativeBanks.swift` holds the other rules. The directory names Skatbank and Ordensbank by the banks that hold their Bankleitzahlen, so the search shows them as "VR-Bank Altenburger Land / Skatbank" and "Wiesbadener Volksbank / Ordensbank" and takes the brand and "apoBank" as the short name of their Bankleitzahl, as it does for DKB.

Consorsbank and DAB BNP Paribas are brands of one bank and share the FinTS server `brokerage-hbci.consorsbank.de` (#227). `Sources/FinTS/Consorsbank.swift` holds the rules of the assistant, and `FinTSServer.isConsorsbank` in `Domain` knows the server by its full host, as `tanRules` of the helper does, so `brokerage-hbci.other.consorsbank.de` is another server. The directory names 70120400 by the bank and not by a brand, so the search shows it as "Consorsbank / DAB BNP Paribas" and takes "DAB" as its short name. A new access on the server gets the bank name "Consorsbank" for both Bankleitzahlen, and its accounts take the bank name of the access. The server takes the login name without spaces. Only for 76030080 does the app show a stored login name of 11 to 13 digits as the account number and the Berechtigtennummer, such as "1234567890 001", because only the page of Consorsbank names that form. Two rules wait for the bank test of issue #11 and a page of the bank: adding "001" to a login name of 1 to 10 digits, which would count a wrong PIN against the holder 001 when the user has another Berechtigtennummer, and a note for 70120400 that its login is the one of Consorsbank. So 70120400 still gets no hint. The TAN length of the methods 900 and 901 waits for the bank parameters of a real login.

Many banks answer an unknown login name with a PIN error. Only at the first login of a setup can the login name be wrong, so only there `SyncFailure.messageAtFirstLogin(userIDHelp:)` names the user ID as well and repeats the hint. A refresh keeps the message and the rules of a rejected PIN.

### Rules of the savings banks and 1822direkt

`Sources/FinTS/SavingsBanks.swift` holds the rules of the savings banks for the assistant, apart from `LoginHints.swift`, so that the rules of another bank group change no line of it. A bank is on the FinTS servers of the savings banks when the label of its host before ".de" starts with "s-fints-pt-" or is "s-hbci" (`BankDirectory.Entry.isOnSavingsBankServer`). The host counts in lower case and without one trailing dot, as in the helper, and a host with an empty label counts as none. The login hint of a savings bank uses the same test.

- A savings bank may give a login name of 17 digits, and its FinTS server accepts the first 16. `FinTSServer.loginName(_:server:)`, which also holds the rule of the cooperative banks, cuts a login name of exactly 17 ASCII digits to 16 for a server of the savings banks (`FinTSServer.isSavingsBank`). The assistant stores the result through `BankDirectory.Entry.loginName(_:)`, also in "Edit Login Data...", and the connector and `Access.isSameLogin` read every login name through it, so an access that stored all 17 digits sends 16 and is the same login. Any other login name stays as typed, after the spaces at both ends are removed.
- The login hint of a savings bank has a note above the fields: the user logs in with the login name and the PIN of the online banking and sets up a TAN method such as pushTAN or chipTAN there before the first login. `LoginHint.note` carries it, and a hint without a note shows none.
- 1822direkt gives its customers IBANs with the Bankleitzahl 50050201 of Frankfurter Sparkasse, whose entry in the directory leads to the server of Frankfurter Sparkasse. Its own FinTS server has the Bankleitzahl 50050222. `BankLookup.note` tells the user to enter 50050222 when the entry is an IBAN with 50050201. A Bankleitzahl that the user enters or chooses gets no note, because the user then means that bank.

### Rules of Commerzbank and comdirect

`Sources/FinTS/CommerzbankGroup.swift` holds the rules of Commerzbank and comdirect for the assistant, apart from `LoginHints.swift`. Both banks belong to the group by the host of their FinTS address: `fints.commerzbank.de` or `hbci.commerzbank.de` for the 159 Bankleitzahlen of Commerzbank with a FinTS address, and `fints.comdirect.de` for the eight of comdirect (`FinTSServer.isCommerzbank`, `FinTSServer.isComdirect`). The Commerzbank page names no Bankleitzahlen, so its hint follows the host.

- The FinTS server of Commerzbank expects the 10-digit participant number, and customers know an 8-digit number. `FinTSServer.loginName(_:server:)` puts "00" in front of a login name of exactly 8 ASCII digits for a server of Commerzbank, so the setup, "Edit Login Data...", the connector, and `Access.isSameLogin` all see the 10 digits. A login name with a letter, such as an alias, stays as typed.
- The login step of Commerzbank asks for the "Participant number". Its note says that the user requests the access at `https://www.commerzbank.de/hbci` first, because Commerzbank switches on "FinTS/HBCI with photoTAN" separately, also for a user who already uses photoTAN on the website. Its help line names the participant number and the activation, so a rejected first login repeats both through `SyncFailure.messageAtFirstLogin(userIDHelp:)`.
- comdirect keeps its hint "Access number" for the four Bankleitzahlen that its page names, now with a note: set up a TAN method such as photoTAN on the website first, wait about two business days until the FinTS server accepts it, and try again with "Edit Login Data...". The other four keep no hint, so a search for "comdirect" still offers 20041111 first.

### Rules of Deutsche Bank, Postbank, and norisbank

`Sources/FinTS/DeutscheBankGroup.swift` holds the rules of this group for the assistant, and `Sources/Domain/Sync/DeutscheBankGroup.swift` its host tests, apart from the files of the other groups. A bank belongs to the group when its FinTS host ends in `.deutsche-bank.de`, `.postbank.de`, or `.norisbank.de`, in lower case and without one trailing dot (`FinTSServer.isDeutscheBankGroup`). The bank directory has 285 Bankleitzahlen of Deutsche Bank, 14 of Postbank, and 2 of norisbank on these hosts.

- Users copy the login number in groups from the bank card or the letter of the bank, and the FinTS login of these banks has no spaces. `FinTSServer.loginName(_:server:)` removes every space for these hosts, so the assistant stores the login name without spaces, an access that stored spaces sends none, and both forms are one login.
- A login name of exactly 10 digits at Deutsche Bank and norisbank is the branch number of 3 digits and the account number of 7. `Access.shownLoginName` shows it as "123 4567890" in the PIN dialog, the TAN dialog, the progress of a challenge, the account settings, and the field of "Edit Login Data...". Saving the field unchanged stores it without the space again. A Postbank ID and an ID that the user chose keep their form.
- Deutsche Bank asks for the "Deutsche Bank ID", which is the branch and account number without the subaccount number unless the user chose an ID of their own, as its help page on the login field says. Postbank and its business brand FYRST ask for the "Postbank ID", and a business profile for the Postbank ID followed by "#" and the profile name, as the guide of Postbank for finance software says. No public page of norisbank names its FinTS login, so norisbank keeps "User ID".
- FYRST is the business brand of Postbank on the Bankleitzahl 10010010, so the search shows that entry as "Postbank / FYRST" and finds it by both names (`DeutscheBankGroup.brandNames`). The other Postbank entries keep their names. The name "Deutsche Bank / CosmosDirekt" for 37070060 waits for the bank test of issue #11, which shows whether a CosmosDirekt account answers on the FinTS server of Deutsche Bank.
- Deutsche Bank lets the user choose a Deutsche Bank ID of their own, which replaces the branch and account number as the login, and Postbank and norisbank have IDs of their own as well. The group answers an old login with the code 9940 or the warning 3960 and the request "Melden Sie sich im Online-Banking an oder kontaktieren den Support". The helper marks that answer `loginReplaced` on the hosts of the group, and the connector maps it to `SyncFailure.loginReplaced`, whose message shows the text of the bank and says to enter the new login with "Edit Login Data...". The PIN may still be right, so the app keeps it and asks for no new one. A refresh with the old login cannot work, so the automatic refresh leaves the access out until the user refreshes it by hand, and the error popover offers "Edit Login Data...". The same answer from another bank keeps the failure of its code.

### Fintech banks and their brands

Many fintech accounts have a German IBAN on a Bankleitzahl that the bank list of hbci4java lacks, or lists under the name of the bank that runs the accounts (issue #233). The Bankleitzahl 20220800 of Banking Circle, TF Bank, and Vivid is missing, so a valid IBAN of it got "The Bankleitzahl is invalid.", and 12070088 of PayPal is "Deutsche Bank (Gf intern)". The bank list of hbci4java 4.1.17 names no FinTS address for any of these Bankleitzahlen.

`Sources/FinTS/Fintechs.swift` holds one table of names by Bankleitzahl, as `CooperativeBanks.brandNames` and `DeutscheBankGroup.brandNames` do. `BankDirectory` shows an entry of the bank list under the name of the table and keeps its place, BIC, and FinTS address, and it adds each Bankleitzahl of the table that the bank list lacks as an entry without a place, a BIC, and a FinTS address. So the search finds "tide", "kontist", "pleo", "qonto", and the other brands, and an IBAN of these banks gets the message of a bank without a FinTS server, which names the extension and the import of a file into an offline account. No login starts, because no entry of the table has a FinTS address. If a later bank list gives one of these Bankleitzahlen an address, the entry keeps it. A name of the table keeps the bank that the bank list names in front, so 12070088 is "Deutsche Bank / PayPal" and the search finds it by "deutsche bank" and by "paypal". Lists outside hbci4java name a FinTS address for 12070088, so the app must not treat it as a bank without FinTS on its own. The only other entries that the bank list names are 50110800 "J.P. Morgan" and 50110801 "J.P. Morgan, Internal Reference", both without a FinTS address, and "J.P. Morgan / Pleo" keeps the name of the bank in front. The other Bankleitzahlen of the table are not in the bank list.

The table holds only the names and Bankleitzahlen of issue #233. The places stay empty, because the issue names none, and the rows of the first step and of "Supported Banks..." leave an empty place out.

The table is code and not a line of `BankData/corrections.properties`. That file corrects the bank list that the helper reads for its dialogs with the banks, and it reaches the app only after the workflow "Bank data" published it and the app downloaded it, and only while the list is not older than the jar of the build. A Bankleitzahl without FinTS means nothing to the helper, and the app must know it in every build and without a network. The brand names were code already.

The Bankleitzahlen file of the Deutsche Bundesbank would add every missing Bankleitzahl with its place, and is left for later: the app would ship and update a second list, which is a new mechanism, for banks that the app cannot reach anyway.

On 2026-11-18 no page of the bank said either. ### Rules of Baader Bank and the brokers on it

`Sources/FinTS/BaaderBank.swift` holds the rules of Baader Bank for the assistant (#226). The bank directory lists Baader Bank as 70033100 with the FinTS address `https://fints.baaderbank.de`, and brokers whose securities accounts Baader Bank keeps log in on the same server. ### Rules of Oberbank

`Sources/FinTS/Oberbank.swift` holds the rules of Oberbank (#236), apart from the files of the other groups. ### Rules of PayCenter and VIMpay

`Sources/FinTS/PayCenter.swift` holds the rules of PayCenter GmbH (issue #238). The bank directory names the Bankleitzahl 70017000 "PayCenter GmbH" in Freising, with the FinTS address `https://fints.paycenter.de`. VIMpay is the consumer brand of PayCenter on the same Bankleitzahl, so the search shows the entry as "PayCenter / VIMpay" and finds it by both names (`PayCenter.brandNames`), as it does for Postbank and FYRST.

PayCenter and VIMpay log in with different login names: the issue names the email address of a PayCenter account and the mobile phone number of a VIMpay account. No public page of PayCenter or VIMpay confirms these logins or their format, such as a phone number with or without the country code. Only the help page of another finance app names the phone number. ### The steps and the keyboard

The line under the title names the step and its number, so that "Back" visibly leaves the login data. The field of the new step takes the keyboard focus: the login name, or the PIN when the login name is filled in. After "Back" to a chosen bank, no field takes it, so a login name typed by mistake does not replace the bank. The login name stays when the user goes back and forth.

### A migrated account takes over the access

The moneymoney-migration spec ("Connect a migrated account to its bank") lets the assistant attach a bank access to a migrated account with the same IBAN, or the same account number and Bankleitzahl, in the same currency. An account of a bank whose access is gone is connected in the same way. The third step shows such an account as "Connects", and it keeps its ID, name, group, and transactions. A closed account is never connected, so the open "Giro" wins over a closed "Giro (alt)" with the same IBAN. When several open accounts match, the row offers "Connect to" with "New account" selected, and the setup connects only the account that the user chooses. An account with an access and an offline account are shown as present and cannot be selected.

`Ledger.setupMatch(for:listed:)` compares two accounts by IBAN when both have one. Else it compares the account number and Bankleitzahl, and also `remoteID` when the account in the app has one, because FinTS keeps the subaccount number only in `remoteID`. So a second subaccount of a connected account number is new. A migrated account has no subaccount number. When it matches more than one account of the listing, each of those rows offers it under "Connect to", and the setup never connects it on its own. An account that was connected without an IBAN keeps the account and subaccount number as its `remoteID`. When the bank lists it with an IBAN later, the IDs differ and the setup lists it as new, because the listing with the IBAN no longer names the subaccount number.

### A second setup of a login

`Store.add(_:state:accounts:)` looks for an access with the same Bankleitzahl and login name. When it finds one, the new accounts join it, so one login keeps one session and one TAN for each refresh. `SetupSession.logIn(holding:_:)` runs the login of such a setup in `SyncCoordinator.exclusively(for:_:)`: it waits for a running refresh of the access, and a refresh that a command asks for meanwhile waits for the login. So the bank never gets two dialogs for one login. The session starts only after the login got the access, so a login that is canceled while it waits leaves the TAN progress and the PIN of the running refresh alone.

The access keeps its stored state, and `Store.add(_:state:accounts:)` ignores the state of the setup. The refresh that waited for the login starts from the stored state and can end before or after "Done". With the state of the setup, "Done" would roll back the state of that refresh, or the refresh would roll back the state of "Done".

### Errors after the login

A second "Next" during the login sends nothing, so the bank gets the PIN once. When "Done" cannot store the accounts, the assistant stays at the third step and shows the error of the database, so the login and its TAN are not lost.

### Look for new accounts

"Look for New Accounts..." on an account of a FinTS access logs in like the setup, with `SetupSession.logIn(holding:_:)`, so it waits for a refresh of the access and a refresh asked for meanwhile waits for it. It uses the stored state, and `SetupSession` without a PIN of its own takes the PIN of the access: the PIN in memory, the saved PIN, or the PIN sheet, as an attended refresh does. A PIN that the user typed is kept, and saved on request, only after the login succeeded. The login stores the new state of the access, and hands it to a waiting refresh with `SyncCoordinator.update(_:state:)`, before it lets the refresh go. The bank and its BIC for the new accounts come from the bank directory, which loads while the login runs, with `BankDirectory.bank(of:)`. The assistant opens at its account step with the notices of the bank and the accounts whose `Ledger.setupMatch(for:listed:)` is not "present", all selected, or says that there are no new accounts. `SetupListing` keeps every account that the bank listed, also those that the step does not show, and "Done" matches the chosen ones against all of them. So a migrated account without a subnumber stays a choice for a new subaccount when the app has the other subaccount of its number already. "Done" without new accounts marks the notices of the bank as shown, as "Done" of the setup does. A login that fails shows the message of the bank with the notices before it, which `BankNotices.setupFailed(_:from:)` marks as shown, as a failed login of the setup does. "Edit Login Data..." shows them in the same way under the error of its login step. "Done" adds them with `Store.add(_:state:accounts:)`, which joins them to the access and keeps its state. The command does not apply to the service of an extension: "Add Account..." with the service runs its setup, which adds only the accounts that the app does not have.

### Edit login data

"Edit Login Data..." opens the login step of the assistant with the login name of the access, an empty PIN, and "Save PIN" on when a PIN is saved. "Done" logs in like the setup, with `SetupSession.logIn(holding:_:)`, but with the ID of the access and without its stored state. Only after the bank accepted the login, and while the login still holds the access, does `Store.updateLogin(of:loginName:state:savedPIN:)` store the login name, the new state, and the saved PIN in one transaction: the PIN of the login when "Save PIN" is on, and none when it is off. `SessionBroker.adopt(_:for:saved:)` takes the same into memory, and `SyncCoordinator.update(_:state:)` hands the new login to a refresh that waits. The coordinator hands the state of a refresh to `SyncCoordinator.storeState`, which stores it at once, before it lets the next work of the access go, so the state of the edit always comes after it. A rejected PIN ends the login with the message of the bank and removes the saved PIN of the access, as any rejection does, so no refresh sends it again.

The login without the state is what sets an access up again after an aborted authentication or a new TAN method: the bank sends its parameters, a new system ID, and its TAN methods. The codes of the banks for an access that must be set up again differ, so `SyncFailure.mayAskForNewSetup` holds for every message of the bank and every unfinished authentication that ends the refresh of the whole access. The helper sends such a message as the failure of the whole access also when the bank ends the dialog after the login, and the app cannot tell the two apart, so the command is offered then too. `RefreshError.suggestsEditLoginData` keeps it with the error of each account, and the error popover then offers "Edit Login Data...". A failure of one account means that the login worked, so it does not.

## Deviations from the spec

- The bank directory of hbci4java holds no field names, formats, or notes. The login step asks for "User ID" and "PIN", except for the five banks of `LoginHint`, and has no customer ID and no format checks. The five banks ask to leave the customer ID empty.
- The country menu is not built. A valid IBAN of another country gets the message that the app finds banks only by a German IBAN or Bankleitzahl so far, and the search covers only the German bank directory. In v0.1 the menu would change nothing: the bank directory has only German banks, and the extensions declare no country for their services.
- The bank directory marks no outdated Bankleitzahl, so the app cannot say that one is outdated. For the entry of a merged bank it shows a note and goes on, as "A Bankleitzahl of a merged bank" describes.
- "Access with" is not shown, because FinTS is the only method until E6.
- The progress text is "Setting up Accounts..." without the current step, because the helper reports no progress while it lists accounts.
- The switch of the access method is not built. It needs a second access method for one bank, which comes with "Access with".
- The bank directory holds no notes, so the login step shows no note of the bank before the first login. The help of the first step says how a credit card is added.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- The first lookup of an assistant starts a helper process for the bank directory. The first step waits 0.4 seconds after the last keystroke before it looks up.
- The bank directory comes with hbci4java and is as current as its release. A new or renamed bank appears with the next update of hbci4java.
- A login hint can go out of date when a bank changes its login. Each hint names its source page, so it can be checked again.
