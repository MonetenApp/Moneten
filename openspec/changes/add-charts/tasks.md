## 3. Pending transactions count

- [ ] 3.1 Write the tests first in LedgerTests and BalanceChartTests. With an initial balance of 1.000,00 € and a pending transaction of -30,00 € in "Groceries" today, the computed balance and the bar of today are 970,00 €, and the withdrawals of the pie hold 30,00 € in "Groceries". A booked version on the same day leaves every bar unchanged. A booked version two days later raises the bars of the two days before it to 1.000,00 € and leaves 970,00 € from its day on. With an initial balance of 500,00 €, two pending rows of -3,20 € with `needsReview` and one booked row of -3,20 € with `needsReview` give 496,80 €. Replace the test "Balances leave out pending transactions".
- [ ] 3.2 Change `Transaction.isInSum(of:)` in `Sources/Domain/Models.swift`, which #139 adds, to `(!isPending || !needsReview) && self.currency == currency`. `Account.isInBalance`, which #139 adds, and `Ledger.sum(ofCategory:in:)` call it, so `Ledger.computedBalance` and the sidebar amount of a category follow. In `dailyBalances` in `Sources/Domain/Charts.swift`, drop `!t.isPending` from the guard and keep the account lookup, so that `Account.isInBalance` decides alone. Without that, the bars stay booked-only: with an initial balance of 1.000,00 € and a pending row of -30,00 € today, the computed balance is 970,00 € and the bar of today 1.000,00 €. Task 3.2 of the change `add-categories` is the same edit. In `periodSums` and `categoryTotals` in `Sources/Domain/Charts.swift`, which sum every currency and skip every pending row today, skip only a pending row with `needsReview`.
- [ ] 3.3 Rewrite the test `pendingRows` in `Tests/StorageTests/OpeningBalanceTests.swift` first, without a second test: a first refresh with a bank balance of 2.500,00 €, booked rows of 700,00 €, and a pending row of -30,00 € gives the initial balance 1.800,00 € and a last bar of 2.470,00 €. Keep `balanceDateBeforeNewestRows` as it is. Then add `Account.bookedSum(of:through:)` in `Sources/Domain/Models.swift`, the sum of the rows with `!t.isPending && t.currency == currency && t.bookingDate <= day`, and let `Account.deriveInitialBalance` subtract it from the bank balance, without `Account.isInBalance`. Task 1.12 uses the same function. Without that change, task 3.2 makes it store 1.830,00 € and a last bar of 2.500,00 €.
- [ ] 3.4 Write a test in MoneyMoneyMigrationTests that guards the filter of `MoneyMoneyMigration.initialBalance`, which #142 adds: booked rows in the account currency with a migration source ID. A source account with a balance of 1.000,00 €, booked rows of 250,00 €, and a pending row of -30,00 € gets the initial balance 750,00 € and, after task 3.2, the computed balance 970,00 €. The code needs no change, because that filter does not call `Account.isInBalance`. Stored initial balances of migrated accounts already follow this rule and need no repair.
- [ ] 3.5 Change `docs/data-model.md` with task 3.2. The row `isPending`: a pending transaction counts toward the balance and the category sums, except one with `needsReview`. The paragraph on `Account.reportedBalance`, which #142 adds, says that the other accounts show `initialBalanceMinor` plus their transactions in the account currency, pending ones included except those with `needsReview`. The sentence on the initial balance of an account that the setup adds keeps "booked transactions".
