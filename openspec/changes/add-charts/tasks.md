Verified by LedgerTests "A bank balance that the booked rows miss proposes a calibration, and a pending row or an account without a bank access proposes none" (1.200,00 € against 1.250,00 € proposes and fills in 1.200,00 €, a pending row of -50,00 € proposes nothing) and "A row booked after the day of the bank balance proposes nothing, and a calibration fills in the balance of today and moves the initial balance by the difference" (500,00 € for 10.11.2026 proposes nothing on 12.11.2026, 450,00 € fills in 420,00 € and sets the initial balance to 650,00 €). Both failed before the code. `Ledger.proposedCalibration(of:today:refresh:calendar:)` compares through `Account.initialBalanceMeetingBankBalance(of:calendar:)`, so it uses `Account.bookedSum(of:through:)` at the day of `bankBalanceDate`, or today without a date, as the derived initial balance does. The harness command `sheet calibration` opens the window as the button does. While the refresh of the access runs, and after it failed for the account, the header proposes nothing: SyncCoordinatorTests "A refresh whose transactions fail proposes no calibration while it runs, nor after it ends, and the next good refresh proposes one" (initial balance 200,00 €, a booked row of +1.000,00 €, bank balance 1.200,00 €, then a refresh with 1.150,00 € whose transactions fail) failed before the fix with -50,00 € during the delivery and at the end of the refresh. The debug harness shows the notice hidden while a refresh of the fake bank runs and back after it ends. The perf scenario "open-account-with-calibration-proposal" opens an account with a proposal among 50,000 rows. "Calibrate" in the account settings still fills the initial balance from the bank balance and does not open this window yet.

## 3. Pending transactions count

- [x] 3.1 Tests for pending rows. Verified by LedgerTests "A pending transaction counts in the balance, the bars, and the pie" (970,00 € for the balance and the bar, 30,00 € of "Groceries" in the withdrawals) and "A pending transaction marked for review counts nowhere, a booked one marked for review counts" (bars of 500,00 € and 496,80 €), and by BalanceChartTests "A booked version on the day of its pending row leaves every bar as it was" and "A booked version two days after its pending row raises the bars between them and keeps the rest". They replace "Balances leave out pending transactions", and each failed before task 3.2 except the one for review, which guards the exception. The scenario "The bank drops it" is verified by BankAccessTests "A refresh asks from the oldest pending row, so it removes a deposit older than the overlap that the bank dropped": the bars of 01.11.2026 to 10.11.2026 rise from 500,00 € to 1.000,00 € when the refresh removes the deposit of -500,00 €.
- [x] 3.2 `Transaction.countsInSums` holds the rule `!isPending || review == nil`, and `Transaction.isInSum(of:)` adds the currency to it. `Ledger.computedBalance`, `dailyBalances`, and the sidebar amounts of the categories, now `Ledger.sidebarAmounts(today:)`, follow through `Account.isInBalance` and `Transaction.isInSum(of:)`, and `periodSums` and `categoryTotals` test `Transaction.countsInSums`. `dailyBalances` no longer skips pending rows in its guard. Verified by the tests of task 3.1 and by MoneyMoneyMigrationTests, whose balances of the account with the pending row "Kiosk" of -10,00 € now end 10,00 € below the balance of the source.
- [x] 3.3 `Account.bookedSum(of:through:)` sums the booked rows in the account currency up to a day, and `Account.deriveInitialBalance` subtracts it from the bank balance. Verified by OpeningBalanceTests "Pending rows do not count toward the opening balance, and the last bar counts them on top of the bank balance": a bank balance of 2.500,00 €, booked rows of 700,00 €, and a pending row of -30,00 € give the initial balance 1.800,00 € and the last bar 2.470,00 €. `balanceDateBeforeNewestRows` passes unchanged. `Account.initialBalanceMeetingBankBalance(of:)` holds the subtraction for `Account.deriveInitialBalance` and for "Calibrate" in the account settings, which subtracted the computed balance and so would have pulled pending rows into the initial balance: LedgerTests "The initial balance that meets the bank balance subtracts the booked rows up to the day of that balance".
- [x] 3.4 Verified by MoneyMoneyMigrationTests "Pending rows of the source stay out of the initial balance and count on top of it": a balance of 1.000,00 €, booked rows of 250,00 €, and a pending row of -30,00 € give the initial balance 750,00 € and the computed balance 970,00 €. The code did not change.
- [x] 3.5 `docs/data-model.md` says that pending transactions count, except those with `review != nil`, for `isPending`, for the balance of the other accounts, and on top of the balance of the source for migrated accounts. The sentence on the account that the setup adds keeps "booked transactions".
