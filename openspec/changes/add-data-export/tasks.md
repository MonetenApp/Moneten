- [x] Write a Gregorian date in every region, without an era and without direction marks (#466). macOS gives this app `en_TH` for English and the region Thailand, and the pattern of that locale for its Buddhist calendar added "BE", so the date read "03/11/2569 BE". Following "Decided for Moneten" in the proposal, `TransactionCSV` now formats every date in the Gregorian calendar with the order and the separators of the locale, removes the direction marks U+200E, U+200F, and U+061C of the Arabic patterns, and `AmountParser` reads the Arabic decimal separator "٫" of an Arabic export as the decimal mark. The separator now counts only once and with no comma or point after it, and a comma or a point before it separates thousands. AmountParserTests "The Arabic decimal separator is a decimal mark only: once, with no comma or point after it" and "Refuses an Arabic decimal separator that occurs twice, has a separator after it, or stands with the Arabic thousands separator", TransactionDraftTests "A typed Arabic decimal separator is the decimal mark, never a group of thousands", and ExportRoundTripTests "A file whose Arabic decimal separator occurs twice or has a point after it is refused, never read as a group of thousands" failed before.
