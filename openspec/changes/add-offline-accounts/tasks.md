## 3. Balance and signed amount

- [x] 3.1 Write the test first in LedgerTests: an offline account in euros with an initial balance of 50,00 €, the transactions 100,00 €, -4,80 €, and -12,00 €, a pending transaction of -10,00 €, a pending transaction of -5,00 € with `review != nil`, and a transaction of -10,00 USD has the balance 123,20 €. The code is `Transaction.isInSum(of:)`, which #139 adds and which `Account.isInBalance` calls, and task 3.2 of the change `add-charts` changes it for pending rows. This change adds no second filter. Done in #272 with LedgerTests "An offline account adds its initial balance and its rows in its currency, pending rows included, except one marked for review".
- [x] 3.2 Replace the choice between "Withdrawal" and "Deposit" in `App/Sources/Views/Sheets.swift` with one signed amount field. `save()` stores the value of `AmountParser` with its sign, without `abs` and the factor of `isDeposit`. `load()` shows a stored amount with its sign. `AmountParser` keeps a leading minus already, so verify it in the app: -4,80 stores -4,80 €, 50,00 stores 50,00 €, and editing a row of -12,00 € shows -12,00. Done in #272, verified in the debug app.
- [x] 3.3 Write the tests first for the fields of an offsetting entry in `Domain`, with the values of the scenarios of "An offsetting entry stays in its currency", "Fields of an offsetting entry", and "Category of an offsetting entry": name, purpose, comment, category "Cash" with `categorizedBy` `.manual` that `Categorizer.categorize` keeps against the rule for "Fees", booking date and value date 05.11.2026, and counterparty DE02120300000000202051. A source in "Uncategorized" gives `categorizedBy` `.none`, and the rule for "Geldautomat" sorts the entry into "Cash". A category restricted to "Business" ends in "Uncategorized" without a matching rule and in "Household" with one. A source account without an IBAN gives no counterparty account. For a row of -200,00 USD, "Dollar Cash" in US dollars can take the entry and "Wallet" in euros cannot. A row of -50,00 € and a row of -200,00 USD selected together leave neither "Wallet" nor "Dollar Cash". Done in #278: `Tests/DomainTests/OffsettingEntryTests.swift`.
- [x] 3.4 Build the offsetting entry and the choice of offline accounts in `Domain` so that the tests of task 3.3 pass. Task 1.10 uses both for the menu. Done in #278: `Ledger.offsettingEntries(of:into:now:)` and `Ledger.offsettingTargets(for:)`. #272 marks the entry with `isManual`, verified by OffsettingEntryTests "An offsetting entry has the opposite amount...".
- [x] 3.5 Amount limit of a typed amount (issue #324). `AmountLimitTests` "One amount holds at most 10.000.000.000.000,00 €" and `EditTransactionTests` "An amount whose minor units do not fit" failed while `Money(fitting:currency:)` took the bounds of `Int64`, so the amount 92.233.720.368.547.758,07 passed the transaction sheet. Both pass now. The signed amount field of #272 reads the amount with `TransactionDraft.amountMinor(in:region:allowsZero:)`, which refuses an amount above the limit with `Money(fitting:currency:)`, verified by TransactionDraftTests "An amount of zero, text that is not a number, an empty field, and an amount beyond the limit are refused".

Verified by ManualRowAmountLimitTests (#272).
