A portfolio with transactions keeps its transaction list (proposal). Verified by PortfolioHoldingsTests "A selection shows positions only when it holds portfolios and nothing else", "A portfolio with transactions, such as one taken over with its rows, keeps its transaction list", and "The ledger takes over the positions of a merge, and lists those of several portfolios by name without regard to case", and by the debug harness snapshot of "Depot" with four rows and no transaction list.
- [ ] 1.2 Fetch positions through FinTS. Built: the helper sends the positions of every page of the custody account balance (HKWPD) in the result line, the connector maps them to `Holding`, and a position that does not read fails only its account. Verified by FinTSHelper self-test "securities account with its positions" and "a position without a name, a price day, and a pair of currencies sends no rate", FinTSConnectorTests "Reads the lines that the helper writes for … securities accounts …" and "A result that does not read fails only its account", and the live check with `scripts/fake-fints-helper.sh` (login "depot"). - [x] 1.3 Take positions from extensions. `ResultMapping` maps `securities` to `Holding` with the fields of the web banking API: `amount` is the value in the account currency, and without it the value is the quantity times `price`, converted with `exchangeRateOfPrice`; `currencyOfQuantity` makes the quantity a nominal amount, and both rates of a nominal amount read `currencyOfQuantity`. A purchase price in another currency converts with `exchangeRateOfPurchasePrice` into a price of one unit in the account currency, also for a nominal amount. A position priced in another currency without a rate keeps its value in that currency, and the balance leaves it out with a notice; the account still counts as refreshed. The merge gives the positions of a setup the ID of the new account. Verified by ResultMappingTests "portfolioValueIsTheSumOfItsSecurities", "securityWithoutAmountIsWorthItsQuantityTimesItsPrice", "priceInAnotherCurrencyConvertsWithTheCurrentRate", "nominalAmountIsValuedAtAPercentage", "nominalAmountInAnotherCurrencyConvertsWithTheCurrentRate", "purchasePriceConvertsWithTheRateOfThePurchase", "nominalPurchaseConvertsWithTheRateOfThePurchase", "priceInAnotherCurrencyWithoutARateAddsNothingToTheBalance", ExtensionConnectorTests "positionWithoutARateIsANoticeAndTheAccountRefreshes" and "setupKeepsTheNoticeOfAPositionWithoutARate", and PortfolioHoldingsTests "The positions of a setup, fetched before the account existed, belong to the account they merge into".

- [x] 1.43 Purchase price of OLB and Bankhaus Neelmeyer (#237). At a FinTS host that ends in ".olb.de" or is `hbci.neelmeyer.de`, `OLBGroup.purchasePrice` reads the purchase price of a position as the purchase value of the whole position: 1.000,00 € for 10 units is 100,00 € per unit, and 9.800,00 € for the nominal amount 10.000 is 98 %. A nominal amount in another currency converts the purchase value with the exchange rate of the position first, so 8.000,00 € for 10.000 USD at 1,25 USD per euro is 100 % and gains 1.000,00 € on a value of 9.000,00 €. Without a rate that links the two currencies, or without the currency of the nominal amount, the position keeps 0,80 € per unit of the nominal amount, which gives the same gain. A purchase price in percent stays as it is, a position with the quantity 0 has none, and another bank keeps the price of the bank. The connector applies it before the check of the amount limit. Verified by OLBGroupTests "At OLB and Bankhaus Neelmeyer, the purchase price of the bank is the purchase value of the whole position", "A bond in US dollars bought for 8.000,00 € at 1,25 USD per euro has the purchase price 100 % and gains 1.000,00 €...", "A bond in US dollars bought for 8.000,00 € without a rate that links euros and dollars keeps the price of one unit in euros...", and "Another bank keeps the purchase price of the bank".
