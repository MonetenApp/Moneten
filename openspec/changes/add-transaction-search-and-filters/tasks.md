# Tasks

One task per requirement of the spec. A task is checked only when a test or a check in the running app shows the behavior.

## 1. Requirements

- [x] 1.1 Results while typing. Verified by the list filters on every change of the search field.
- [x] 1.2 The search covers all text of a transaction. Verified by LedgerTests "The search finds the category name and the IBAN and the account number of the own account, also after a change": "groceries", "uncategorized", the IBAN, and the account number find the rows, and after renaming the category to "Food" and changing the IBAN and the account number the new texts find them and the old ones do not. The search text holds the name of the category of the row, or "Uncategorized" without one, and the IBAN and the account number of the own account.
- [x] 1.3 The search finds text inside words. Verified by LedgerTests "The search finds text inside words...".
- [x] 1.4 The search ignores case, umlauts, and Unicode form. Verified by LedgerTests "The search finds text inside words...".
- [x] 1.5 All search words must occur. Verified by LedgerTests "The search finds text inside words...".
- [x] 1.6 The search accepts the rule language. Verified by LedgerTests "The search accepts the rule language, and a term without a field name searches every searched field inside words" (`name:"Stadtwerke" AND amount<-100`, `"Lohn" OR "Gehalt"`, phrases in order, wildcards, `purpose:/^Lohn/`, exact amount ends), "A word without a field name finds text inside words, also with a wildcard, and a rejected text searches its words" (`Groc*`, `/groceries/`, `Girok*`, IBANs with and without spaces, `"REWE-Mar`, `REWE OR`, `name:`), "A phrase finds its words across any white space, and a quoted word finds text inside words, which a rule does not", "An amount term beyond the range of an amount compares as a rule does", and "An amount term compares the amount in the decimals of its currency". `Rule(parsing:)` stays as it is, and the search reads rejected text with the lenient mode of its tokenizer. "Search for" puts the name in quotation marks. LedgerTests "A field term and a regular expression with a field name match the rows that the same rule matches" guards that the search reads a field as a rule does. "Search for" quotes the parts of the name between its quotation marks, verified by "Search for finds the row it starts from, also with quotation marks in the name". LedgerTests "The search true finds every row, and false finds none" proves `true` and `false`, and "Typing a field term, a regular expression, or an amount searches the plain words until the text reads" proves the rejected texts of the spec, among them `Stadtwerke or`, `Stadtwerke +`, `Name:Stadtwerke OR`, `name:="Stadtw`, `Stadtwerke amount:=-`, and the excluded words in `Stadtwerke -"Gehalt` and `name!=Gehalt "Stadtw`. "Rejected text reads open quotation marks, regular expressions, and groups to the end, and leaves out the words it excludes" proves `Stadtwerke NOT (Gehalt OR Lohn`, `Stadtwerke -"Abschlag November`, `Stadtwerke NOT /Lohn Oktober`, and `Stadtwerke /Absch.*`. PerformanceTests in a release build, fastest of three, four runs at a load average of 6 to 9: over all 50,000 rows no search 4 to 5 ms, `kundennummer 7` 12 to 13 ms, `Kunden*` 13 ms, `name:Haendler*` 15 to 18 ms, `name:Haendler1*` 12 to 13 ms, `purpose:Rech*` 18 to 19 ms, `/Rechnung 1/` 31 ms, and `/\d{5} Kunden/`, which every row reads, 80 to 84 ms; over the 37,500 uncategorized rows `Haendler1*` 9 to 10 ms, `name:"Haendler1*"` 10 ms, `amount<-50` 8 ms, and `/Haendler1/` 18 to 19 ms. Building the ledger takes 111 to 113 ms.

- [x] 1.15 Amount filter. - [x] 1.20 Saved searches. Verified by SavedSearchTests "A saved search shows the withdrawals that contain its text from every account", "A saved search keeps its category and account filters, because its filter bar shows both menus", "A saved search holds no rows, so a new matching transaction shows the next time", and "A saved \"30 Days\" counts from the day of the selection, and a removed category comes back as Show All", by SavedSearchStoreTests "Saved searches stay after a restart in their order, and a rename and a removal stay too" and "A rename keeps a filter bar that this version cannot read byte for byte", by BrowseHistoryTests "After A, S, and B, removing S lets Back open A and leaves B as the only forward step", and by AllDataTests for the complete export, "The complete import numbers the saved searches from 0 in their order, so the next saved search goes last", and the refusal of the complete import. Verified in the app with the debug harness on the `seed` data: "REWE" with "Withdrawals" and "90 Days" saved as "Groceries at REWE" and "Tankstelle" saved as "Fuel" both come back after a restart. Opening "Groceries at REWE" from "Tagesgeld" shows 11 transactions of all accounts with the count and the sum, and the filter bar shows "Period", "Status", "Amount", "Category", and "Account". Opening "Fuel" from "Groceries" with "Tagesgeld" in the "Account" menu sets the account filter to the "Show All" of the saved search. In the saved search, "Groceries" and "Girokonto" in the two menus both stay, and going to "Girokonto" then resets only the account filter. A rename and a removal stay, and removing another entry keeps the open one. A saved search keeps an account group (#312): SavedSearchTests "A filter bar saved with an account reads as before, and a group goes into its own key" and "A saved search whose account group was removed opens without the account filter", SavedSearchStoreTests "A saved search stored with an account keeps it, and one with an account group keeps the group after a restart", which reads a filter bar as the first version wrote it, and AllDataTests "A saved search keeps its period, its filters, and the ends of its amount range as decimal text" and "A saved search with both an account and an account group is refused with its place". In the app, "REWE" saved with "Private" opens from "History" with 58 transactions and "Private" in the "Account" menu, and "Bulk only" saved with "Bulk" opens with "Show All" after the group "Bulk" is removed.
- [x] 1.21 The search is fast with a large history. Verified by PerformanceTests: 10 ms for a search over 50,000 transactions in a release build.
- [x] 1.22 Turning the pin off resets the filters. Verified in the app with the debug harness: with the pin on, a custom period, "Unchecked", and the search "REWE" in "Girokonto", turning the pin off shows "Show All" in every menu, an empty search, and all 162 transactions of "Girokonto". The saved filter bar is removed, and after a restart every menu shows "Show All".
- [x] 1.23 Turning the pin on saves the filter bar. Verified by LedgerTests "The pin saves the period and the filters, and not the search text", "A saved category or account that no longer exists comes back as Show All", "A saved \"30 Days\" counts from the day of the launch, and a saved month stays that month", and "The pin saves an amount range as its decimal ends, and the next launch reads them back". Verified in the app with the debug harness across restarts on one data folder: turning the pin on saves "90 Days" and "Unchecked", a later change to the custom period 01.04.2025 to 31.03.2026 saves again, and the next launch shows the pin on with that period and its dates, "Unchecked", and "Groceries". A saved category that the user removed comes back as "Show All". With the pin off, "90 Days" does not survive a restart. With the pin on, "Unchecked", and the custom amount -500 to -100, the next launch shows both and not the search text.
- [x] 1.24 A view resets the filter it does not show. Verified by LedgerTests "A view resets the filter that its filter bar does not show", and in the app with the debug harness: from "Girokonto" with "Unchecked" and "Groceries" to "Rent", the status stays and the category filter is "Show All". From "Rent" with "Tagesgeld" in the "Account" menu to "Girokonto", the status stays and the account filter is "Show All". - [x] 1.25 A regular expression without a field name. Verified by LedgerTests "A word without a field name finds text inside words, also with a wildcard, and a rejected text searches its words": `/groceries/` and `/^girok/` find the row, and `/DE02 1203/` and `/DE02120300000000202051/` do not. LedgerTests "A field term and a regular expression with a field name match the rows that the same rule matches" guards that the check of the plain text before the regex call drops no row that the rule matches.
- [x] 1.26 A regular expression that does not end. Gate round 2 proof in LedgerTests "A stopped regex discards all rows in either order, also with NOT" and "One search adds the processor time of all regex calls". Each stopped search returns no rows with `gaveUp`, and the 100-row release case stops at about 300 ms of processor time. The Debug harness snapshots in [the PR gate evidence](https://github.com/MonetenApp/Moneten/pull/172) show "The regular expression stopped because it is too slow" without a sum, in light and dark mode. `scripts/check-debug-log.sh` exercises a stopped search and rejects its text in the log.

## Gate round 2

- [x] Stop results atomically and expose the outcome. LedgerTests `stoppedSearchHasNoPartialRows` rotates all positions for the Astra and Opus cases, also with NOT. The Debug snapshots show the exact stop message without a sum.
- [x] Share 300 ms of processor time across all regex calls of one search. LedgerTests `searchRegexProcessorBudget` checks 100 literal slow texts and the result. Three final release runs take 304.64 to 312.06 ms on the searching thread.
- [x] Compile out-of-range amount comparisons to constants for each currency exponent. LedgerTests `searchAmountAtInt64Edges` proves the requested equality and inequality at `Int64.max`, both stored edges, and agreement with the rule.
- [x] Keep fallback constants and lower supported performance budgets. LedgerTests `unfinishedSearchConstants` proves the literal false input and negation. The two faster regex cases use 200 ms. The exhaustive case retains 600 ms because concurrent compilation produces 233 to 254 ms. [The PR gate evidence](https://github.com/MonetenApp/Moneten/pull/172) records every measurement and load average.
