# Tasks

One task per requirement of the spec. A task is checked only when a test or a check in the running app shows the behavior.

## 1. Requirements

- [x] 1.1 Results while typing. Verified by the list filters on every change of the search field.
- [x] 1.2 The search covers all text of a transaction. Verified by LedgerTests "The search finds the category name and the IBAN and the account number of the own account, also after a change": "groceries", "uncategorized", the IBAN, and the account number find the rows, and after renaming the category to "Food" and changing the IBAN and the account number the new texts find them and the old ones do not. The search text holds the name of the category of the row, or "Uncategorized" without one, and the IBAN and the account number of the own account.
- [x] 1.3 The search finds text inside words. Verified by LedgerTests "The search finds text inside words...".
- [x] 1.4 The search ignores case, umlauts, and Unicode form. Verified by LedgerTests "The search finds text inside words...".
- [x] 1.5 All search words must occur. Verified by LedgerTests "The search finds text inside words...".
- [x] 1.6 The search accepts the rule language. Verified by LedgerTests "The search accepts the rule language, and a term without a field name searches every searched field inside words" (`name:"Stadtwerke" AND amount<-100`, `"Lohn" OR "Gehalt"`, phrases in order, wildcards, `purpose:/^Lohn/`, exact amount ends), "A word without a field name finds text inside words, also with a wildcard, and a rejected text searches its words" (`Groc*`, `/groceries/`, `Girok*`, IBANs with and without spaces, `"REWE-Mar`, `REWE OR`, `name:`), "A phrase finds its words across any white space, and a quoted word finds text inside words, which a rule does not", "An amount term beyond the range of an amount compares as a rule does", and "An amount term compares the amount in the decimals of its currency". The rule parser stays as it is. "Search for" puts the name in quotation marks. LedgerTests "A field term and a regular expression with a field name match the rows that the same rule matches" guards that the search reads a field as a rule does. "Search for" quotes the parts of the name between its quotation marks, verified by "Search for finds the row it starts from, also with quotation marks in the name". LedgerTests "The search true finds every row, and false finds none" proves `true` and `false`, and "Typing a field term, a regular expression, or an amount searches the plain words until the text reads" proves the rejected texts of the spec, among them `Stadtwerke or`, `Stadtwerke +`, `Name:Stadtwerke OR`, `name:="Stadtw`, `Stadtwerke amount:=-`, and the excluded words in `Stadtwerke -"Gehalt` and `name!=Gehalt "Stadtw`. "Rejected text reads open quotation marks, regular expressions, and groups to the end, and leaves out the words it excludes" proves `Stadtwerke NOT (Gehalt OR Lohn`, `Stadtwerke -"Abschlag November`, `Stadtwerke NOT /Lohn Oktober`, and `Stadtwerke /Absch.*`. PerformanceTests in a release build, fastest of three, four runs at a load average of 6 to 9: over all 50,000 rows no search 4 to 5 ms, `kundennummer 7` 12 to 13 ms, `Kunden*` 13 ms, `name:Haendler*` 15 to 18 ms, `name:Haendler1*` 12 to 13 ms, `purpose:Rech*` 18 to 19 ms, `/Rechnung 1/` 31 ms, and `/\d{5} Kunden/`, which every row reads, 80 to 84 ms; over the 37,500 uncategorized rows `Haendler1*` 9 to 10 ms, `name:"Haendler1*"` 10 ms, `amount<-50` 8 ms, and `/Haendler1/` 18 to 19 ms. Building the ledger takes 111 to 113 ms.
